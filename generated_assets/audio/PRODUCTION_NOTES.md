# Audio stream: production notes

Stream: audio. Branch: `assets` only. Everything in this folder is pending owner review (`approval_status: pending_review`).

## What is here

## How it was made

- **Method.** Every note and every sound is generated by code in `source/tfjaudio/`: band-limited oscillators (PolyBLEP saw and square), additive piano and bells, FM plucks, hybrid string and brass sections built from detuned voices with ensemble and filter envelopes, modal synthesis for metal, glass and drums, filtered noise models for air, fire, water and debris, and synthetic reverbs. No recordings, no sample libraries, no AI music or audio service. Rebuild with `python3 source/produce_music.py --isolated --jobs=2` and `python3 source/produce_sfx.py --jobs=2`.
- **Musical identity.** One six-note motif (1, b6, 5, b3, 4, 1 over a 3+3+2 clock rhythm) runs through every cue. Its answer phrase ends on the second degree, so phrases feel unresolved, as the story is. A major inversion ("hope") appears in the success cues and the finale. Chapter keys and tempos: Helix D minor 112 BPM, VITA E minor 92, Nova Grid C minor (Phrygian) 120, Civitas B minor 100, Meridian D minor (Neapolitan colour) 128. Each chapter has its own colour: Helix cold FM arpeggios and server ticks, VITA glass and breath textures, Nova Grid metallic percussion and reese bass, Civitas pulse bass and alarm tones, Meridian temporal glitches and shimmering clusters with cameos of all four.
- **Adaptive set per chapter.** The prep, tension and combat loops and the critical-asset layer share tempo, key, length (64 bars) and phrase structure to the sample, so the game can cross-fade between intensities in sync or play the critical layer on top (Godot `AudioStreamSynchronized` or two players started together). The boss track has an 8-bar intro played once and a 64-bar body that loops from `loop_offset_seconds`.
- **Seamless loops.** Loops are rendered so they are periodic by construction: every note, reverb and delay tail that runs past the loop end is folded back onto the start, and bus and master processing run on a tiled copy from which the middle cycle is taken (cycle-to-cycle difference about -127 dB). The 48 kHz master is converted to 44.1 kHz with FFT resampling, which treats the signal as periodic, so the loop stays seamless after the rate change. QC decodes the delivered Ogg, joins its end to its start, and compares that wrap with the continuous master: the error at the wrap equals the codec's own error on similar material elsewhere in the file and there is no high-frequency burst at the join (see `seam` in `cue_sheet.json`). For the short SFX loops the reference is stricter: three cycles encoded back to back, error measured at the internal boundary.
- **Mixing and mastering.** Per-part loudness auto-gain by role, phrase-level automation, sidechain ducking under kicks and hits, a 26 Hz high-pass, gentle bus compression (1.7:1) and a 4x oversampled look-ahead true-peak limiter. Music is normalised on the delivered file to -16 LUFS integrated (cinematic scores -16.5, which leaves a little room under narration), true peak at or below -1 dBTP measured after Ogg encoding. Critical-asset layers sit at about -19 LUFS on their own because they are always heard on top of a bed; `qc_extra.py` checks the summed pair.
- **SFX loudness.** Each sound is normalised to its family's momentary loudness (BS.1770 K-weighted, 400 ms, measured as played, so mono counts on both speakers): weapons and explosions -14 to -10 LUFS-M, impacts and debris -18 to -14, UI -22 to -18, foley about -24 to -22, ambience -24 LUFS integrated. Peak ceiling -1.3 dBTP before encoding, checked again after encoding (files whose codec overshoot passes -1.05 dBTP are re-encoded slightly lower). The limiter never takes more than about 4 dB. Very spiky sounds (arcs, small debris, fibre snaps) have only their spikes soft-saturated at 4x oversampling first (`transient_compression_db` in the QC record). Quiet families (UI, foley, alarms) peak below -3 dBFS because the loudness target takes priority over the peak window; that is intended. One-shots have a DC high-pass at 18 Hz, at most 1 ms lead-in, and their natural decay ends inside the file.
- **Variations.** Frequently repeated sounds have 3 to 5 variations (different pitch, resonances, debris patterns and timing) at matched loudness; pick one at random per play.
- **Phones and headphones.** Low-end weight is doubled by distorted mid layers (300 Hz to 3 kHz) so impacts still read on phone speakers; stereo width is moderate and mono-compatible (SFX mono except explosions, shockwave, secondary blast, collapse, critical assets and the larger UI cues).

## Hook ids and proposals

- `sound_id`, `music_cue` and `ambience_cue` follow ASSET_CONTRACT section 15. Variants share a `sound_id`.
- Cues without a contract `music_cue` carry a proposed value in `notes` only (not in the field): `<chapter>_prep`, `<chapter>_critical`, `<chapter>_theme`, `<chapter>_transition`, `title`, `teaser`, `timeline_shift`, `mission_success`, `mission_fail`, `chapter_complete`. Proposal for the coding agent and stream 1.
- SFX without a contract `sound_id` (charge, travel loop, breach prep and arm, shockwave, metal stress, debris, UI error, unlock, upgrades, achievement, foley) are layer or animation-event sounds; `used_by` says so.
- `mission_success` and `mission_fail` are on the UI layer sounds `GAM-SFX-UI_VICTORY-01` and `GAM-SFX-UI_DEFEAT-01`; the longer music results are `GAM-MUS-VICTORY-MISSION`, `GAM-MUS-DEFEAT-MISSION` and `GAM-MUS-VICTORY-CHAPTER`.

## Rights

Original algorithmic composition produced for the owner; no third-party samples. Every sound and note is generated by the code in `source/`, written for this project; no recordings, sample packs, loops, presets from commercial libraries or AI music or audio services were used. `rights_status` says "pending owner review" because the owner has not reviewed the files yet. The legacy `assets/audio/music/gameplay_bed.ogg` and `assets/music/` were not touched; their rights stay unverified (the legacy bed was only measured for loudness comparison).

A different texture, such as sung vocals, a choir with words, or the feel of a real live orchestra or soloist, cannot be made convincingly with this synthesis method. It would need a service such as Suno or ElevenLabs used on the owner's commercial plan, with the terms saved as evidence. Those services were not used.

## Known limits

- The string, brass and choir-like sounds are synthesised and read as a "hybrid cinematic" sound, not as a recorded orchestra.
- Loudness was measured with pyloudnorm and an in-house BS.1770 momentary meter; the validator's optional `--loudness` (ffmpeg ebur128) can confirm it independently.
