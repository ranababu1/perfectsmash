## Full description (limit 4000) — 2251 chars

```text
Temporal: First Jump is an offline tactical destruction puzzle. A lone operative is sent back through time to stop five institutions whose systems will one day end civilisation. Your tools are a handful of weapons, an arc preview and real 2D physics.

HOW IT PLAYS
Every mission is a destructible facility with a critical core. Study how it is held up, choose a weapon, aim with the arc preview and bring it down. Supports give way, structures collapse and chain reactions spread. Optional objectives, such as finishing within a shot count, reward efficient play.

100 HAND-AUTHORED MISSIONS IN FIVE CHAPTERS
I. Helix Systems, 2089: AI server facilities
II. VITA Genomics, 2092: gene laboratories
III. Nova Grid, 2096: an energy basin
IV. Civitas, 2100: a surveillance city
V. Meridian, 2104: a temporal convergence facility

SIX WEAPONS, EACH WITH A JOB
Kinetic Accelerator and Breach Charge from the start, then a new weapon in each later chapter: the Penetrator, Pulse Cannon, Disruptor and Temporal Lance. Each reacts differently to materials. Energy cells overload, electronics burn out, shield emitters fall to the Disruptor, and temporal crystal yields only to the Lance.

OBJECTIVES AND UPGRADES
Every mission has a primary objective and optional ones. Earn Mission Points and Research Data, then spend Research Data on upgrades: three levels for each weapon attribute. Upgrades make destruction more efficient and are never required.

A STORY TOLD IN STILLS
Narrated-style still-image cinematics with subtitles (no voice acting) tell the story: an opening, chapter introductions and endings, and a finale. Replay them any time from the Story Archive.

FEATURES
- 100 missions, five chapters, six weapons
- Real 2D physics with supports, collapses and chain reactions
- Arc preview aiming
- Primary and optional objectives
- Weapon upgrades bought with Research Data
- Story cinematics with subtitles and a Story Archive
- Settings for sound, music and vibration
- Landscape play, single player

FULLY OFFLINE, NOTHING TO SIGN UP FOR
No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No leaderboards. No analytics. No online features. Your progress stays on your device.

Note: the story contains no voice acting; all narration is shown as subtitles.
```
