# Chapter II - VITA Genomics, "The Price of Perfection" (vita_01 - vita_20, campaign levels 21-40)

Weapons: Kinetic Accelerator, Breach Charge, and the new Penetrator (introduced in vita_01-03 on glass and
rack lines, then combined). Curve (recommended shots): 01-05 2-3, 06-10 3-4, 11-15 3-4, 16-19 4-5, 20 = 6 (staged:
expose, breach, finish; all three weapons; the chapter's widest facility). Probe result for every row: stand check
OK and every recorded solution wins 3/3 within the recommended shots (intended line exactly at it).

Rules in play: a collapse leaves a critical asset at 1 hp, so every dropped vault needs a finishing hit; the
Penetrator also passes through destroyed components, so lines of glass/racks fall to one shot and difficulty is
carried by concrete faces and static dividers (Penetrator cannot pass) that need Breach lobs (reach about 900 px
from the muzzle) or a collapse. Shots behind a concrete wall must be lobbed at a point, not at a component.
