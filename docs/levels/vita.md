# Chapter II - VITA Genomics, "The Price of Perfection" (vita_01 - vita_20, campaign levels 21-40)

Weapons: Kinetic Accelerator, Breach Charge, and the new Penetrator (introduced in vita_01-03 on glass and
rack lines, then combined). Curve: 01-05 establish (2-3 shots), 06-10 combine (2-3), 11-15 deepen (3),
16-19 mastery (2-4), 20 finale (4). Probe result for all rows: stand check OK, every recorded solution wins 3/3,
intended line within recommended shots. Notes: the Penetrator also pierces destroyed components, so lines of
racks and glass fall to one shot; difficulty is mostly carried by cover (concrete faces, statics) that needs
Breach lobs or a structural collapse. Several collapses are lethal to a core regardless of health_scale, so
cores that sit on a structure use health_scale to keep direct fire slower than the weak point.
