# Chapter V: Meridian, "The Convergence" (levels 81-100)

Mission files `meridian_01` .. `meridian_20`. Facility: a hidden subterranean convergence complex (violet and indigo light, temporal reactors, stabilisers, anchors, monolith slabs, phase shields, hybrid server/lab/reactor structures). The core is the `convergence_core` (an energy component: it bursts with a secondary blast).

**Weapons.** New: Temporal Lance (straight, dead-flat, passes through up to three components and also strikes a fourth; the only weapon that damages `temporal_crystal`; 2-3 charges per mission). Every earlier weapon is available (Kinetic, Breach, Penetrator, Pulse Cannon, Disruptor) and is used where its skill fits.

**Curve.** 1-5 establish the Lance (2-3 shots); 6-10 combine it with the older skills (2-4); 11-15 deepen with layered, multi-target sequences (3-5); 16-19 mastery (3-6); 20 is the finale (the largest facility in the game, all weapons).

**Authoring notes.**
- Crystal parts (`phase_shield`, `temporal_cell`, `anchor_column`, `timeline_relay` of `temporal_crystal`) are immune to everything but the Lance; they also block kinetic lines, so the Lance is the answer to a crystal wall or a crystal support.
- A Lance round hits up to four components in its line (three pierces and the last), and flies dead straight from the muzzle (226, 512). Cores stand on pedestals or off the line so one Lance does not clear a level by accident.
- Kinetic accuracy falls off beyond about x = 1250; targets farther away are given to the Lance, Pulse Cannon or Penetrator.
- A Breach lob reaches only about x = 1100 at ground height from the operative.
- Every `convergence_core` is `reactor_housing` (260 hp, survives most falls, large secondary blast) with a scaled `health_scale` (0.3-0.5 floor by level band) so a core takes two or three decisions, not one.
- `shielded_by` emitters are used for cores that must be exposed by a collapse or a chain.
- Shot limits in the optional `max_shots` goal are recommended + 1 or 2.
