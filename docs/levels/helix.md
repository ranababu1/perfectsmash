# Chapter I: Helix Systems, "The Machine That Decided" (levels 01-20)

Weapons: Kinetic Accelerator and Breach Charge only. Curve (authoring guide section 7): 01-05 establish, 06-10 combine (2-4 shots), 11-15 deepen (3-5), 16-19 mastery (3-6), 20 finale (4-8, largest facility). Reference missions: helix_02..05. Probe result is `solve <id> 3` (wins/3, shots of the intended line).

Physics notes learned while authoring (kept for the next engineer): a Kinetic round does about 77 impact damage after drop-off, so lightweight alloy takes 2 hits, electronic racks 2 (1 with `health_scale` 0.9), glass 1, composite 3-4, reinforced steel is effectively immune; a component driven to the critical state is released to physics, and a deck with `min_supports` falls whole when too few supports remain. The probe aims at the lowest lift that clears an obstacle, so targets must not sit just behind a thin edge (the real round drops a few px below the preview); lobs reach roughly x <= 1150 and need low walls (<= 72 px) or a far target to clear with margin.
