# Perfect Smash: Privacy Policy

_Effective date: to be set when first published · Applies to Perfect Smash for Android (package `dev.imrn.perfectsmash`)_

**Summary: Perfect Smash does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device data.**

## What the app stores, and where

The game keeps a small save file on your device only. It contains:

- which levels you have completed, your best star rating and fewest shots for each;
- your settings (sound, vibration, aim guide);
- simple per-level play counters (attempts, wins, losses, timeouts) used for the optional play report.

This file never leaves your device. It is not backed up to a cloud service by the app, and it is deleted when you
uninstall the game. You can erase your progress at any time with **Settings → Reset progress**.

## No network access

The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It contains no advertising,
analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

## Permissions

- **Vibrate:** short vibrations when you hit a target or clear a level. You can turn this off in Settings.

No other permissions are requested.

## The optional play report

**Settings → Copy play report** puts a short text summary (level numbers, the counters above, stars and the aim
guide setting) on your clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically. It only goes somewhere if you choose to paste it
into a message yourself. It contains no name, contact details or device identifiers.

## Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding optional analytics, this policy will be updated
before that version is released and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
